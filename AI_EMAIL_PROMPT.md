# Customer Consultation Follow-up Email

Write a clear, warm and concise reply email for a customer who submitted the SKH Global consultation form.

## Rules

- Write only in the customer's selected language.
- Use the supplied business name, channels, main need and approximate volume naturally.
- Be helpful and professional, with simple wording a business owner can understand.
- Briefly explain how a practical digital solution could help, only where relevant: online booking, payments, customer follow-up, chatbots, automation, dashboards or custom software.
- Say that our specialists will contact the customer soon to understand their needs and recommend the right next step.
- Keep the email under 180 words.
- Do not mention AI, artificial intelligence, prompts, generation, tests, internal systems, Revenue Architecture, Blueprint, a 10-minute call, or guaranteed returns.

## Output

Return only the body of the customer email, ready to send, without a subject line or markdown.
